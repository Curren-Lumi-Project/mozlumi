Welcome to Mozlumi

Mozlumi is a Japanese input method for macOS that guesses romaji typos and offers the corrected conversion. It also has AI conversion that runs on your Mac.

  • All conversion runs on your Mac. Mozlumi uses the network only to check for and download updates.
  • Installing requires your Mac's administrator password.
  • You do not need to log out or restart.
At the end of the installation, "Running package scripts" may seem to stop for a while. The installed files are being verified; please wait.

Mozlumi is an unofficial input method based on Mozkey, which is based on Mozc. It is not affiliated with Google or Mozkey.
